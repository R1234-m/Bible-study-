This Week’s Study

Faithful, peaceful, and simple Bible study for the week ahead.

A Catholic and Orthodox-friendly format built for two one-hour gatherings on Tuesday and Thursday.

Tuesday Session Thursday Session

Opening Prayer

“Lord Jesus Christ, Son of the living God, we come before You with grateful hearts. Open our minds to Your holy Word, warm our hearts with Your love, and strengthen us to follow You more faithfully. Amen.”

Tuesday · 1 hour

The Beatitudes: The Way of the Kingdom

Matthew 5:1–12

  • Reflect on humility, mercy, meekness, and peace.
  • Notice how the Kingdom of God is shaped by character, not status.
  • Ask which beatitude speaks most clearly to your life this week.
Discussion:

Where do you see pride, fear, or hardness of heart in yourself?

Thursday · 1 hour

The Lord’s Prayer and a Life of Prayer

Matthew 6:9–13

  • Explore reverence, trust, forgiveness, and daily dependence on God.
  • See how prayer shapes both the heart and the day.
  • Choose one line of the prayer to carry into your week.
Discussion:

What part of the prayer feels most challenging or most needed right now?

12-Week Program

Follow a steady path of prayer, reflection, and growth.

Week 1
Matthew 5:3-12
Tuesday: Study the Beatitudes and the call to humility and mercy.
Thursday: Reflect on how these blessings shape a faithful life.
Week 2
John 15:1-8
Tuesday: Explore remaining connected to Christ through prayer and obedience.
Thursday: Consider how fruitfulness grows from abiding in God.
Week 3
Psalm 23
Tuesday: Reflect on God as shepherd, guide, and comforter.
Thursday: Discuss peace, trust, and the goodness of God.
Week 4
Philippians 4:4-9
Tuesday: Study joy, gratitude, and the peace of God.
Thursday: Practice focusing the mind on what is good and holy.
Week 5
Ephesians 4:1-6
Tuesday: Talk about humility, gentleness, and unity in the body of Christ.
Thursday: Reflect on how unity is lived through patience and love.
Week 6
James 1:2-8
Tuesday: Consider trials as opportunities for endurance and wisdom.
Thursday: Discuss trusting God in seasons of difficulty.
Week 7
Luke 10:25-37
Tuesday: Study the meaning of mercy and compassion.
Thursday: Reflect on practical ways to love our neighbor.
Week 8
Matthew 6:9-13
Tuesday: Pray through the Lord’s Prayer line by line.
Thursday: Consider how prayer shapes the heart and daily life.
Week 9
Romans 12:1-2
Tuesday: Reflect on offering ourselves to God as living sacrifices.
Thursday: Discuss resisting worldly patterns and seeking holiness.
Week 10
Colossians 3:12-17
Tuesday: Study compassion, kindness, humility, and patience.
Thursday: Reflect on letting peace and gratitude rule in the heart.
Week 11
1 Corinthians 13
Tuesday: Focus on love as the greatest gift and the highest calling.
Thursday: Discuss how love is shown in speech, patience, and service.
Week 12
Revelation 21:1-7
Tuesday: Reflect on hope, renewal, and God’s promise of a new creation.
Thursday: Close the study by looking at endurance and faithful hope.

Helpful Materials

  • A Catholic or Orthodox study Bible
  • A notebook or journal
  • A quiet place for prayer
  • Optional: a short reflection from a Church Father

May your study be grounded in peace, wisdom, and love.
