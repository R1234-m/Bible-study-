This Week’s Study

Faithful, peaceful, and simple Bible study for the week ahead.

A Catholic and Orthodox-friendly format built for two one-hour gatherings on Tuesday and Thursday.

Tuesday Session Thursday Session

Opening Prayer

“Lord Jesus Christ, Son of the living God, we come before You with grateful hearts. Open our minds to Your holy Word, warm our hearts with Your love, and strengthen us to follow You more faithfully. Amen.”

Tuesday · 1 hour

The Beatitudes: The Way of the Kingdom

Matthew 5:1–12

  • Reflect on humility, mercy, meekness, and peace.
  • Notice how the Kingdom of God is shaped by character, not status.
  • Ask which beatitude speaks most clearly to your life this week.
Discussion:

Where do you see pride, fear, or hardness of heart in yourself?

Thursday · 1 hour

The Lord’s Prayer and a Life of Prayer

Matthew 6:9–13

  • Explore reverence, trust, forgiveness, and daily dependence on God.
  • See how prayer shapes both the heart and the day.
  • Choose one line of the prayer to carry into your week.
Discussion:

What part of the prayer feels most challenging or most needed right now?

Suggested Flow for Each Session

1

Opening prayer

2

Read the passage aloud

3

Discuss key themes

4

Share one application for the week

Helpful Materials

  • A Catholic or Orthodox study Bible
  • A notebook or journal
  • A quiet place for prayer
  • Optional: a short reflection from a Church Father

May your study be grounded in peace, wisdom, and love.
